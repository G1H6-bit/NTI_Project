Spaghetti Bolognese

30 MINEASYSERVES 4

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti — 400g
  • Ground beef — 500g
  • Tomato sauce — 2 cups
  • Onion — 1, diced
  • Garlic — 3 cloves

Steps

  • Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook spaghetti until al dente.
  • In a pan, cook ground beef with onion and garlic until browned.
  • Stir in tomato sauce and simmer for 15 minutes.
  • Toss the pasta with the sauce and serve hot.
